skill

todoist-gtd

by spm1001 productivity

Orchestrates Todoist queries with GTD semantics. LOAD BEFORE any Todoist operation — CLI alone doesn't know the user's structure (outcomes are sections, not tasks; team vs personal; 3-tier ontology). Prevents wrong-field queries and missing context. Handles weekly review and pattern detection. Triggers on 'clean up outcomes', 'team priorities', 'is this a good outcome', 'weekly review', 'am I overcommitting', 'check my patterns', 'should I take this on', 'I said yes to', 'scope creep', 'freedom
